VantaView 1.0 is the first full release, for Android phones, tablets, Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV.

**Download:** https://github.com/wardy-uk1/VantaView/releases/latest/download/VantaView.apk (always the newest version)

What's new since 0.7.0:

- **Search:** one search for channels, programmes, films and series, with recent searches and voice search.
- **Library:** your Watchlist, Continue watching, Watched and Reminders in one place.
- **Programme reminders:** Remind me in the TV Guide, with a pop-up, or a notification when VantaView is closed.
- **Sort Movies and TV Series:** recently added, A to Z, Z to A, newest, oldest or top rated.
- **Settings, all new:** Playback, Appearance (dark, black, light or follow device, accent colour, text size), Subtitles and audio, Sources and storage, Parental control, Updates, and Help and about.
- **Subtitles and audio:** pick tracks while watching, preferred languages, subtitle size and colour.
- **AI subtitles (beta):** subtitles made on your device as you watch, or translated into English.
- **Updates:** VantaView checks for new versions and installs them from Settings.
- **Parental control:** hide adult categories, lock Sources, clear watch history.
- **Live player:** swipe for brightness and volume (boost up to 200%), sideways to change channel, Audio/Subtitles/Size buttons, and number keys on a TV remote.
- **TV guide for the free channels**, and a new loading screen with a light ripple.
- **TV fixes:** works on Android 7 boxes and Fire OS 6, the country chooser fits a TV screen, slimmer sidebar.

VantaView doesn't supply any channels, films or series. Free software under the GNU GPL version 3 or later.
