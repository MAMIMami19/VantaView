VantaView 0.7.0 adds a TV guide, backup and restore, and a much better phone experience. It installs over 0.1.0 and every test build, and keeps your profiles, playlists and favourites.

**What's new**
- **TV Guide:** a timeline of your channels from yesterday to a week ahead, in the side bar and as a Home tile. Pick a day and category, drag sideways through the day, tap a programme for details and tap the live one to watch.
- **Now and next:** Live TV rows show what's on now (with a progress bar) and what's next. Home has an **On now** row for your favourite channels.
- **Settings, with backup and restore:** back up profiles, playlists and logins, favourites, watch history, add-ons, debrid accounts and settings to one file. Logins are locked with a backup password. Restore by replacing everything or adding what's missing.
- **Automatic backups** after every playlist or profile change, keeping the last 5.
- **Send a backup to your phone** by scanning a QR code on the same Wi-Fi.
- **Free channels (optional):** "Try free channels" adds the Free TV list of officially free-to-air channels. Nothing is added unless you choose it.
- **Portrait player on phones:** the video on top and the category's channels underneath; tap to switch.
- **Swipe up or down** on the video to change channel.
- **Type a channel number** on a remote or keyboard to jump straight to it, and search by number.
- **Long channel lists** get a fast-scroll handle.

**Fixes and tidy-ups**
- The screen follows your phone's rotation instead of forcing landscape.
- Back goes to the previous page instead of closing the app; the player hides its controls first; Home asks before exiting.
- More Xtream providers now load their live channels.
- "Event only" channels stop after 10 seconds with a clear message instead of loading forever.
- Provider divider rows (like "◆◆◆ CINEMA ◆◆◆") show as headings, and "UK|" style prefixes are hidden from channel names.
- The Live TV header no longer squashes on phones.

**Install**
Download `VantaView-0.7.0.apk` and open it on your device (allow installing from this source when asked). On Fire TV, use the Downloader app.

**Known limits**
- Signed with a test key, so it installs over the test builds; a future build with a proper release key will need an uninstall first.
- The TV guide comes from your playlist. Playlists without a guide show "No guide information". The first load of a large guide can take a minute or two.
- Reminders, catch-up, Search and Sports are still to come.
- Automatic backups in the app's own folder are deleted if the app is uninstalled. Choose a folder in Settings to keep them.
