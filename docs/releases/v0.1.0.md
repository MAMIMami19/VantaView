First public release of VantaView, an open-source (GPL-3.0) IPTV & VOD player for Android phones, tablets, Android TV and Fire TV. Same code as test build 15.

**What's in it**
- Profiles with avatars, Kids profiles and a parent PIN
- Home: Live TV, Movies and Series tiles, muted live preview, Continue watching with resume, New season alerts, favourite channels
- Live TV from Xtream Codes and M3U playlists, with favourites, search and choose-your-countries
- Movies and TV Series from Stremio-compatible add-ons (install by link, import from a Stremio account, back up and restore)
- Debrid: Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, TorBox and Premiumize, cached sources first
- Watch history with resume and "Up next"
- Side bar that hides itself on TV

**Install**
Download `VantaView-0.1.0.apk` and open it on your device (allow installing from this source when asked). On Fire TV, use the Downloader app.

**Known limits**
- Signed with a test key, so it installs over the test builds; a future build with a proper release key will need an uninstall first
- Some Xtream providers still show no live channels
- TV Guide, Search, Sports, Library and Settings are still to come

VantaView comes with no channels or content. You add your own sources and are responsible for having the right to watch them.
